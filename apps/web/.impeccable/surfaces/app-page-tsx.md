# Landing page

Scope: the public landing page (`app/page.tsx`): its first screen, the stage under it, and the "how the numbers are made" section before the close. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: property investors, short-let hosts and managers, and agents, in equal weight, finding out what PropSights is and whether it is worth exploring. The page has to say what the three products are (owner, 2026-10-08): the Playground (free dashboard comparing short-lets, long-lets and sales), Reports (written data insights on Cyprus property), and the MCP connector (the same data in Claude). Action: say which place you care about, then open the Playground. Proof: real towns on the first screen, with areas a few streets wide drawn on them and how full and how dear the short-lets inside are; then the visitor's own place, drawn on the map and answered three ways.

Constraints: no testimonials, client names or prices exist; the name is undecided (PropSights in use); nothing delivers a question or request to the team yet; the Playground can be opened on a named area by id but not on a hand-drawn shape; the Playground itself is still in its old dark design, so the landing page cannot show a picture of it; there is no illustrator, so the first screen's picture is made from open map data.

Chosen direction: Start with your place. Memorable moment: typing a town into the first screen and landing on the map below with an area already drawn round it and three products answering for it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The first screen asks the one thing every visitor to a property-data site arrives with, which place, and shows on real towns, one after another, what the answer looks like. It refuses the abstract picture (an outline, a diagram, a glowing map) that stands where a way in or a real figure should be.

OWN-WORLD: Beige paper (#F4F1E8), ink (#161C11), olive (#4A5E3A) for the action and for every drawn area and its line, light orange for what is counted. Google Sans, tabular figures. The picture is a street map, of Limassol, Nicosia, Paphos, Ayia Napa, Larnaca and Protaras in turn, in the page's own map colours (off-white land, soft blue sea, pale olive green, sand-grey buildings and roads) that fades into the paper with no frame. A drawn area is a firm, hard-cornered olive line with a thin white edge under it, a light tint the streets read through, and square white corners with ink borders. Its figures sit on a small white card with a one-pixel ink edge, joined to the area by an ink hairline: the area's name, then a three-line table in 13px tabular figures (short-let: count, a night's price, how full; long-let: count, a month's rent; for sale: count, asking price), the short-let line marked with the dot its listings wear on the map, and the occupancy again as a bar along the card's foot in that shade.

STORY: The visitor reads what PropSights is; watches areas a few streets wide being drawn in real towns, each answered with how full it is and what a night costs; types their own town; lands on the map below with an area drawn round it and the Playground, a report and the Connector answering for it; moves a corner; opens the Playground, or reads how the numbers are made and asks the team.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav. Left, vertically centred: the headline in three lines at about 58px, with "Prop" and "sights" in the light orange, one sentence saying who the team is and what it does, then the search field, 60px tall, which is glass (owner, 2026-10-09: "a nice liquid glass search thing. crystalized"): a clear slab on the paper with a fine cut edge, a polished rim, a light along its top that follows the pointer, and a filled olive "Show me" button inside its right end; nothing under it. Right, filling 58% of the screen from top to bottom and bleeding off the right edge: the drawn map of a town, fading into the paper on its left and under the nav, with three olive areas and a white card pinned beside each: the short-lets, long-lets and homes for sale inside it, counted and priced. It arrives complete, on Limassol. Below 1024px the picture is a band under the words, centred on the area in hand, with that area's card docked in its corner for as long as the band stays on it. Under the picture, one small line: the pause control, the key to the listings' shades, the map credit.

SECOND VIEWPORT: The stage, opening on the whole island with an area round Limassol. Plus and minus at the map's top right enlarge it in steps, a drag moves it (a finger's too, once it is zoomed in), and whenever it is zoomed in a button offers to draw a fresh area where the map now is; a place picked in the first screen's search box arrives already zoomed to, with an area round it.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION AND MOTION: Typing opens a list of places with their listing counts; picking one moves the page down to the stage, which shows the ground round that place with an area drawn round it and the three products answering. The picture runs by itself: a town's map slides in, its listings appear, a pointer draws three areas one after another, and as each closes its listings take their colours and its card is pinned; then the next town. Each area stays about a second to be read before the pointer goes on. It waits while the visitor types and can be paused. With reduced motion the first town stays, complete.

FORM: Search-first, the commonest opening in the product's own field, on the owner's answers to a structured round after the research; the picture's treatment follows the owner's reference (felt.com). Owner-pinned; no roll was run for this first screen. Seed key 7074d5ea belongs to the surface roll that preceded the first island screen and is kept only as that record.
