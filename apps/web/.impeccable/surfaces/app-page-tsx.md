# Landing page

Scope: the public landing page (`app/page.tsx`): its first screen, "How it works" right under it, the products' tiles and chapters, the questions and the close (order since plan 3, 2026-10-17). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: property investors, short-let hosts and managers, and agents, in equal weight, finding out what Plotsights is and whether it is worth exploring. The page has to say what the three products are (owner, 2026-10-08): the Playground (free dashboard comparing short-lets, long-lets and sales), Reports (written data insights on Cyprus property), and the MCP connector (the same data in Claude). Action: say which place you care about, then open the Playground. Proof: real towns on the first screen, with areas a few streets wide drawn on them and how full and how dear the short-lets inside are; then the visitor's own place, drawn on the map and answered three ways.

Constraints: no testimonials, client names or prices exist; the name is undecided (Plotsights in use); nothing delivers a question or request to the team yet; the Playground can be opened on a named area by id but not on a hand-drawn shape; the Playground itself is still in its old dark design, so the landing page cannot show a picture of it; there is no illustrator, so the first screen's picture is made from open map data.

Chosen direction: Start with your place. Memorable moment: typing a town into the first screen and landing on the map below with an area already drawn round it and three products answering for it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The first screen asks the one thing every visitor to a property-data site arrives with, which place, and shows on real towns, one after another, what the answer looks like. It refuses the abstract picture (an outline, a diagram, a glowing map) that stands where a way in or a real figure should be.

OWN-WORLD: Blue Night (owner, 2026-10-17; DESIGN.md is the authority; it replaced direction G "Olive Window"). A navy night card for the hero (night-950 to night-900, a soft blue glow rising behind the town) inset in a white page; navy cards (night-800) for live data and ice or cloud tiles for paper things down the page; the close and footer back in one night card. The night map of a real Cyprus town (land clear, a dark sea, streets and coast in faint dotted ice), on which every short-let is a light in one blue scale (#5A7DA6, #7894B3, #AFC5DC, #EDF3F9), brighter where fuller, its halo added to its neighbours'. Google Sans Flex, headings and large figures at 300 in two tones (ink, then mist on night or slate blue on white); eyebrows over headings and pills for every button, field and tag (owner's picks); glass only where something floats (the cards and the list over the map, the bar by day); no saturated accent.

STORY: The visitor reads what Plotsights is; watches areas a few streets wide being drawn in real towns, each answered with how full it is and what a night costs; types their own town; lands on the map below with an area drawn round it and the Playground, a report and the Connector answering for it; moves a corner; opens the Playground, or reads how the numbers are made and asks the team.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The night card, the bar inside its top (wordmark with ice dots, the tagline from 1280px, four links, the ice pill). Left, vertically centred: the eyebrow "Cyprus property intelligence", the headline "Plot your next move / in Cyprus property. / Insights backed by data." in three thin lines (up to 56px, 300), mist but for "Plot" and "sights" in ink so the name is read out of it; the sentence in two tiers; a pill search field, 58px, with the dark "Show me" pill inside its right end; the database's counts under it. Right, filling 58% of the card top to bottom, frameless and faded at the words' side: the tour of six towns as night maps, three areas drawn in ice, each answered on a night-glass card (from 1280px; between 1024 and 1279px one docked card). A two-line strip at the card's foot: pause, "Next town", the town, the key; the Sample data tag and the credit. Below 1024px the map is a band under the words with the last area's card docked at its top-left.

SECOND VIEWPORT: "How it works" on an ice band (plan 3, owner 2026-10-17: "I love the how it works the section"): "Backed by data. Follow one listing from the ad to your answer.", the three database counts counting up, then one listing followed in four steps beside a held night frame (the island with every short-let a light; the zoom to Kato Paphos; booked told from blocked; a home for sale matched with the lets near it), ending on the three products, whose tiles follow on white, then a chapter each on night and ice bands, the questions as a compact data sheet, and the close (main action "Email the team"). The stage (DrawHero) is not mounted.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION AND MOTION: Typing opens a list of places with their listing counts; picking one moves the page down to the stage, which shows the ground round that place with an area drawn round it and the three products answering. The picture runs by itself: a town's map slides in, its listings appear, a pointer draws three areas one after another, and as each closes its listings take their colours and its card is pinned; then the next town. Each area stays about a second to be read before the pointer goes on. It waits while the visitor types and can be paused. With reduced motion the first town stays, complete.

FORM: Search-first, the commonest opening in the product's own field, on the owner's answers to a structured round after the research; the picture's treatment follows the owner's reference (felt.com). Owner-pinned; no roll was run for this first screen. Seed key 7074d5ea belongs to the surface roll that preceded the first island screen and is kept only as that record.
