# Solutions page

Scope: the public Solutions page (`app/solutions/page.tsx`), reached from the bar's "Solutions" link and the landing's products band ("Explore all solutions"). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: investors, short-let hosts and managers, and agents and advisers, in equal weight, who already know Plotsights exists and want to see everything it offers in depth before choosing. The page explains the four solutions (Playground, Reports, Connector, Custom solutions): what each is, who it is for, what is inside, the questions it answers, how to get it, each with its working thing. Action: the solution's own (Open the Playground, Ask for a report, Ask for access, Email the team); the page's main action is "Email the team" in the close. Proof: the landing's working things on the island's real figures (demo data labelled "Sample data"), the true read cadence and the models.

Constraints: no clients, testimonials, logos, prices, turnaround times, accuracy figures or "real-time"; no "daily" (calendars every 2 days, prices and ads weekly); pricing and tiers are unconfirmed; requests reach the team by email only; the Connector's map links are built but not deployed; the Playground is still direction G, so no picture of it.

Chosen direction: Blue Night, extended (owner's world, DESIGN.md). Owner chose a dedicated page (2026-10-19). Memorable moment: the in-page bar of the four that follows the reader, lit on the solution being read, so no scrolling back up.

Unresolved: whether the bar keeps "Reports" and "Connector" beside "Solutions" (merged here for room); whether the Connector's map links may be claimed once deployed.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One dataset, four ways to use it, ordered from doing it yourself to having it done: each solution is one band with its working thing, so the page proves rather than lists. It refuses the category's product page of identical icon cards, logo walls and zigzag screenshots.

OWN-WORLD: Blue Night as DESIGN.md records it: navy night card hero and close inset in white, bands alternating night and ice, navy cards for live data, white paper on ice for paper things, Google Sans Flex 300 two-tone headings, eyebrows and pills, glass only on what floats (the bar, the in-page bar once stuck).

STORY: The visitor reads that all four rest on one dataset; scans the four by what they do; finds their role's fit; reads each band (claim, what it is, what is inside, questions it answers, who it is for, its action) beside its working thing; sees what the data is and how often it is read; emails the team.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Night card: eyebrow "Solutions", "Four solutions. One dataset." two-tone, the lede, and the four as an index in large thin type, each a verb of how you get it, linking down. The in-page bar under the card.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION AND MOTION: The in-page bar sticks under the site bar and lights the band in view; each working thing plays its one moment once as it arrives (curve drawn, page laid down, first answer streamed).

FORM: code-led; structure pinned by the brief (no roll run: the owner cannot be asked from this run).
