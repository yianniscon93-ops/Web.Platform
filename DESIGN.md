# Design System: Plotsights landing

Scope: the public landing page (`apps/web/app/page.tsx`). Every value here is read from the built page: the landing's roles in the `.bn` and `.bn-night` blocks of `apps/web/app/globals.css` (the `:root` `--th-*` roles above them are direction G's and are read only by the Playground now), then `apps/web/app/landing-hero.css` (the bar, the first screen, the opening), `landing-sections.css` (the bands, the products' tiles and chapters, the questions, the close and the footer), `landing-how.css` ("How it works") and `landing-motion.css` (the scroll motion), and the night map pictures in `apps/web/public/landing/`, whose colours are baked in by `apps/web/scripts/build-hero-town.mjs` and `build-island-map.mjs`. The face is self-hosted in `apps/web/public/fonts/google-sans-flex/` (and `google-sans/` for Greek). The Playground under `/dashboard` keeps direction G for now; it follows Blue Night later (owner, 2026-10-17: "not now").

This is **Blue Night**, the owner's own saved idea (`docs/design/ideas/blue-night/`, from a reference he collected), shown to him as a standalone concept on 2026-10-17 and built the same day at his asking. His picks from the concept: Google Sans in its Flex cut, for the light 300; eyebrow labels and pill buttons on, as in the reference (reversing the earlier ban), with the search a pill too ("rounder"); the town-tour hero, words on the left and a night map of each town on the right with three drawn areas and their short-let, long-let and for-sale cards. It replaces direction G, "Olive Window" (paper, olive and lime, frosted glass; 2026-10-14 to 2026-10-17, in git history at adcc46a).

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Cyprus at night"**

The page is calm, expensive and quiet, a night sky over data: a navy first screen with a soft blue glow rising behind a real Cyprus town, then a white page, then night again. On the night map every short-let the team reads is a light, its brightness its occupancy this season, so a town's fullest streets glow; an area a few streets wide is drawn on it in ice and answered on a dark glass card for all three markets. That map is the page's one rich thing. Everything else gives it room: thin type in two tones, no saturated colour, navy cards and pale tiles instead of borders, pills for everything you press.

The idea, in the subject's own terms: **on this page, every short-let in a Cyprus town becomes a light on its night map, brighter where it is fuller, and every plot drawn on it is answered for all three markets.** A short-let analytics site shows short-lets only and a listings site shows no occupancy; the three-market card on a hand-drawn plot of a named Cyprus street is this product's own.

**Key Characteristics:**
- A navy night card for the hero and another for the close, inset 12px (8px on a phone) from the white page with 20px corners.
- Between them the grounds alternate (plan 3, owner 2026-10-17: "I see a lot of white in the background"): bands of ice and night with the hero card's own inset and corners, 12px apart where two meet, white only for the products' tiles and the questions, never two white sections together. Navy cards for live data, ice for paper things, cloud tiles for lists to choose from.
- One hue of blue for the data, monotone in lightness: on night the fullest is the brightest light.
- Google Sans Flex at 300 for every heading and large figure, two-tone (the statement in ink, the payoff in mist or slate blue); 400 for reading; 500 for labels and buttons; nothing bold.
- Eyebrows over headings and pills for every button, field, tag and chosen option, as in the reference.
- Glass only where something floats: the cards and the list over the night map, the bar once it floats over the white page.

## Colors

Night, day, and one blue scale. No saturated accent: the second tone is mist on night and slate blue on white.

### Night
- **Night 950 to 900** (`night-950` #14191F, `night-900` #18202B): the hero card and the close card, a gradient top to foot (foot to top in the close); the solid fallback of every dark glass surface.
- **Night 800** (#213047): the navy cards on white (the tipping point, the conversation), the listing in "How", the action on white, a read section on the report's rail, booked nights.
- **Night 700** (#2A3B55): the answer bubble, the action's hover on white.
- **Glow** (`glow-600` #2E3D50, `glow-500` #3D5E7F): the glow that rises in the hero and falls in the close, inside every navy card from below; `glow-500` is also the brand's dots on white, the current section on the report's rail, the report's bars, and the How line as it fills.
- **Mist** (`mist-300` #A3AFBB, 7.9:1 on night-950, 6.0:1 on night-800): secondary text and the payoff words on night. `mist-dim` (#8996A5) the key's empty ring and "Sample figures" on a map card.
- **Ice** (`ice-200` #B7C9DD): the action on night (10.4:1 with night on it), a drawn area's line and corners, the brand's dots on night, the visitor's bubble, the slider.

### The map
- **Night map** (`map-sea` #0A0E13 at 55% over the hero's night in the town pictures, `map-sea-island` #0F141A in the island's, `map-line` #B7C9DD for every street, road and coast at its own strength; `map-shore`, `map-road`, `map-road-major` the island's coast and roads): baked into `town-<key>.svg` and `island.svg` by the build scripts; the land is left clear (`map-land` #18202B under the island in the Connector's answer).

### Day
- **Paper** (#FFFFFF): the page, the report's sheet.
- **Ink** (#1A1F26): text on white. **Slate 600** (#4A5563, 7.6:1): secondary text on white. **Mist ink** (#627082, 5.0:1): the payoff words and eyebrows on white; on ice and cloud it is a step darker (`mist-ink-pale` #566476, 4.8:1 on ice), so small text in the second tone holds 4.5:1 there.
- **Ice tile** (#DCE6F1) and **cloud** (#E6E6EE): the pale tiles. **Line** (#DCE2E9): a hairline in a list or a table.

### The data scale
- **Night blues** (`data-1` #5A7DA6, `data-2` #7894B3, `data-3` #AFC5DC, `data-4` #EDF3F9): how full a short-let has been this season, in the page's four classes (under 60%, the 60s, the 70s, 80% and over). One hue, lightness rising, so on night the fullest is the brightest light and every step holds 3:1 on every night ground (the lowest 4.1:1 on night-950, 3.8:1 on night-900, the map's land, and 3.1:1 on night-800; the brightest 15.8:1 on night-950). On white the lowest two hold (4.3:1, 3.1:1) and the two bright steps vanish, so a mark of the scale on white wears a navy ring; better, the scale lives on night.
- **A light**: a solid dot in its class's colour with a soft halo of the same colour, added to its neighbours' so a town glows where it is full (`TownTour.tsx`). The key's four dots, the short-let line's mark on a card and the occupancy bar carry the same glow; nothing else on the page glows.

### Named Rules
**Brighter Is Fuller.** On the night map, a listing's brightness is its occupancy and nothing else: the halos add up, the dots never do.

**Night Carries Data.** Live figures sit on night (the hero map, the tipping point, the conversation); paper things sit on pale tiles (the report, the views, the questions). White text never sits on a pale tile.

**No Saturated Accent.** The page has no colour outside the navy-to-ice range; emphasis is the second tone of a heading, never a coloured word.

**Grounds Alternate.** Below the hero the page is a stack of inset bands (ice for "How it works" and Reports, night for the Playground and the Connector) with white between some of them; white is the minority of the page's height and two white sections never touch. Night next to the night hero was tried and merged into one slab (2026-10-17), so the band after the hero is ice.

**The Page Ends In Night.** The close and the footer share one night card.

## Typography

**Face:** Google Sans Flex (with Google Sans, then sans-serif), self-hosted variable 300 to 700, Latin and Latin Extended. The Flex cut has no Greek, so Greek falls through to Google Sans (self-hosted, 400 to 700, with Greek), the same design: the Connector's Greek question and answer keep their own letters. Pinned by the owner: Google Sans since 2026-10-08, the Flex cut for its 300 since 2026-10-17. There is no second face.

**Character:** thin and two-tone. Headings and large figures at 300, the statement in ink and its payoff in the second tone ("Playground. What any street earns, rents for and sells for."); reading at 400; labels, buttons and figures inside text at 500.

**The scale.** Nothing on the landing page is set under 15px at any width (owner, 2026-10-12).

### Named Rules
**Two Tones, One Face.** Emphasis is the second tone and the size step; never a second family, never bold.

**The Floor.** 15px is the smallest text on the page.

**The Name In The Headline.** The headline is mist but for "Plot" and "sights" (of "Insights"), in the night's ink, so Plotsights is read out of it in order, as the opening has just struck it (owner, 2026-10-17: "make sure the plot and sights strike differently so that the user immidiately understands where the name comes from"). Direction G's device, set in Blue Night's two tones instead of a colour. Nowhere else on the page is a word picked out.

**Eyebrows Inform.** An eyebrow says what the section is for in a few words ("Draw it yourself", "Written by the team", "In Claude", "How it works", "Contact"); it is sentence case at the fine size in the second tone, never tracked capitals.

## Elevation & Depth

Surfaces are told apart by tone: a navy card on white, a pale tile on white, the night card on white. A shadow only on what floats: the cards and the list over the night map (`0 18px 36px -22px`, black at 85%), the report's sheet raised off its tile, the open menu.

### The Glass Rule
Glass only where something floats over something that can be seen through it: the cards pinned on the night map and the list of places (night glass: night-900 at 92%, an 18px blur, a faint light rim and inner highlight), and the bar once it floats over the white page (white at 88%, an 18px blur, a hairline under it). Everything else is solid: the navy cards, the tiles, every button. Without `backdrop-filter`, or with `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce`, each is its solid fill.

## Shapes

Round. Every button, field, tag and chosen option is a pill (owner, 2026-10-17); a question that may take two lines is a 22px-cornered option, the list of places 18px. Corners by kind: 1px a slider's track, 2px a bar's top, 3px a calendar square, 10px the report's sheet, 12px a map card, 14px an answer bubble, 16px a navy card or a pale tile, 20px a night card. Dots everywhere a point is meant: a listing, a corner, a stop on the How line, a mark in a list.

## Motion

Calm and smooth (owner, 2026-10-17: "I like the motion. if it can be smoother I prefer it"). Long, soft ease-outs (travel `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.7, 0.2, 1)`, fades an ease-out cubic), short travel (8 to 28px, scale no lower than 0.985), staggers of 80ms so neighbours overlap and flow, never more than six in a run. Only opacity, transform, clip-path and a stroke's dash move; nothing reflows (CLS 0). No scroll listener and no library: CSS scroll timelines and one small module (`Reveal.tsx`, `app/landing-motion.css`).

- **The opening** (first page of a visit, never with reduced motion; `Opening.tsx`, `lib/landing/opening.ts`): the name is struck in two. "Pl" and "t" rise and the pointer draws the plot between them as the "o": *Plot*, in ink. A beat after the line closes, *sights* comes into focus in mist (0.64s, a slight blur and slide). While it does, the pointer walks to the map; the whole name holds (at least 1.1s after the "o", 0.48s after "sights"), then rises to the nav, "sights" turning to the name's ink on the way, and the headline's lines rise out of masks with "Plot" and "sights" lit. The words land about 3s in; any scroll, key or press ends it.
- **The tour:** areas drawn corner by corner, lights brightening in a wave, cards pinned; it waits out of view or while the visitor types, pauses on request, holds its first town with reduced motion.
- **The glow** breathes, 9s (11s in the close), opacity and a 4% swell; still with reduced motion. In the close it also rises with the scroll.
- **Arrivals** (`data-rv`): headings, sentences, frames and tiles rise in once as they arrive; a heading's second tone fades in a beat after its first. Allowed under three guards: the server sends everything visible; the module hides only what lies wholly below the window once the page has loaded; nothing is hidden without script, with reduced motion, or in print.
- **Moments** (`data-moment`, once, as a block is a third in view): the tiles' rows slide and bars grow; the frame's tipping mark rises; the report's page is laid down, its rail fills to the section showing, its months grow; the Connector's first answer streams in; the questions' rows come in; the month of step 3 fills a night at a time and its blocked nights turn hatched; the name in the close rises and its plot is drawn again. A block already in view when the page loads is simply shown as it is.
- **Count-ups:** the three database counts and the island's short-lets count up from 86% of themselves, in tabular figures, true figures only. The number is set to its start only while out of sight, and screen readers are given the true figure throughout.
- **Tied to the scroll** (from 1024px, motion welcome, scroll timelines): the story's picture changes with its step (a short crossfade with a 12px rise), the island zooms to Kato Paphos and the town comes up, its area is drawn, the match lines run out, the rail fills; the Playground's curve draws as its card is scrolled in. Where scroll timelines are missing (Firefox today) or on a phone, the same moves play once in time as each part arrives; the two never meet on one element.
- **Reduced motion:** no hiding, no travel, no scrubbing. Every part in its final state (the town, the month split, the lines drawn), counts at their number, the story's picture changing with its step without a fade, the Connector's first answer whole.
- **Feedback:** buttons press to 0.97 and their arrows move 3px; links' underlines lift; a question row's toggle turns 45° and the row opens in 0.36s where the browser can size to content.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- Keep live data on night and paper things on pale tiles.
- Set headings and large figures at 300 in two tones.
- Alternate the grounds below the hero: ice and night bands, white in between at most.
- Show all three markets wherever an area is summed up ("don't mislead the customer", owner, 2026-10-09), and say "Sample data" wherever a figure is demo data.
- Give each section one working thing built from real data, usable with a thumb and readable without its script.

### Don't:
- Don't add a saturated colour, a coloured word, or bold type.
- Don't put glass where nothing floats, or a shadow on anything that neither floats nor lies raised (the map cards, the list, the open menu, the report's sheet are the only ones).
- Don't let the map, a light, an area or a card under the words.
- Don't use monospace labels, logo tiles, or tracked capitals.
- Don't count up a figure that is not the database's own, or hide anything that is in view, above the window, without script, with reduced motion, or in print.
- Don't let two white sections touch, or put night beside the night hero.
