What we know

Every listing on the island, every week, since 2024.

We don’t sample. We scrape the full short-let calendar and every long-let and sale listing, then normalise it into one weekly, listing-level table. That is what the Playground and every report run on.

Short-let
Airbnb calendars
~14.9k active listings, availability & nightly price every 48h; effective occupancy modelled from bookings, not blocks.
Long-let & sales
Bazaraki listings
Asking rents and prices with geolocation, so short-let revenue can be set against buy-side and rent-side yield.
Grain
Listing × week
~880k listing-weeks. Any polygon, any week range, resolves in milliseconds — that’s why drawing is instant.
Method
Honest numbers
Medians over means, effective over raw occupancy, freshness shown on every page. If a number is forward-looking, it says so.
