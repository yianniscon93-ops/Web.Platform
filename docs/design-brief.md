# RealSights — Landing Page Design Brief

## What is RealSights?
RealSights is a property intelligence platform for Cyprus. It gives investors, STR hosts, and real estate professionals access to the most complete data on short-term rental performance across all districts of the Republic of Cyprus — occupancy rates, pricing trends, booking patterns, and interactive market maps.

**Value proposition headline:**
> "The most complete data platform for Cyprus properties at your fingertips."

**Business model:** Freemium. The platform is free to access with core features. Premium tiers unlock cross-market comparison, deeper analytics, and exports. The goal of the landing page is to get visitors to sign up for free, then convert them to paid.

---

## Target Audience
Three equal personas:
1. **Property investor** — researching which district or property type to invest in for STR
2. **Existing STR host** — benchmarking their performance against the market
3. **Real estate agent / property manager** — advising clients with data

---

## Color Palette

| Role | Hex |
|------|-----|
| Dark background / navbar | `#323D42` |
| Primary brand / CTA buttons | `#3F778C` |
| Secondary accent | `#3B524C` |
| Mid-tone cards / borders | `#6587A1` |
| Subtle backgrounds / hover | `#8FA9B5` |
| Light sections / whitespace | `#C0D0D0` / `#FFFFFF` |

Cool, professional, no warm tones. The palette should feel like a premium data product — not a travel or lifestyle brand.

---

## Typography
Crystal clean, modern sans-serif. Reference: [airroi.com](https://www.airroi.com). Think Inter, Geist, or DM Sans — high legibility, wide letter spacing on headings, thin weights for body text. Nothing decorative.

---

## Competitor Reference
[AirROI](https://www.airroi.com) — study their layout, section structure, how they present data previews, and their CTA patterns. We want a similar level of polish but with our own palette and a stronger emphasis on interactive data exploration as you scroll.

---

## Landing Page Structure

### Section 1 — Hero
- Full-width, dark background (`#323D42`)
- Strong headline: "The most complete data platform for Cyprus properties at your fingertips"
- Sub-headline: something about STR occupancy, pricing, and market trends across all Cyprus districts
- Two CTAs: **"Explore for free"** (primary, `#3F778C`) + **"See how it works"** (ghost/secondary)
- Background: a subtle animated or static Cyprus map heatmap, or a blurred screenshot of the dashboard

### Section 2 — Interactive Map
- This is the flagship feature — the actual map is **embedded and interactive on the landing page**
- Visitors can explore the Cyprus district map directly, see occupancy or pricing overlays without signing up
- Nudge: certain data layers or drill-downs show a "Sign up to unlock" prompt inline
- Label: "Explore the market. Every district. Every month."

### Section 3 — Market Analysis Preview
- Show a scrolling or tabbed preview of what market analysis looks like
- Occupancy over time chart, average nightly rate by bedroom count, top-performing areas
- Some data is visible, some blurred with lock icons (clear free vs paid distinction)
- Label: "Understand where the money is — before you commit"

### Section 4 — Feature Breakdown (scroll reveal)
Present features one by one as the user scrolls, each with a visual:
1. **Occupancy tracking** — live availability data, updated every 2 days
2. **Pricing intelligence** — average nightly rates by area, room type, season
3. **Market comparison** — [PAID] compare districts side by side
4. **Listing deep-dives** — [PAID] individual listing performance and benchmarks
5. **Data exports** — [PAID] download CSVs for your own analysis

Use a visual treatment that clearly labels free vs paid (e.g., a small "Free" or "Pro" badge on each feature card).

### Section 5 — Pricing Tiers (light treatment)
- Keep simple at landing stage: Free / Pro
- Free: map access, district-level summaries, 3-month history
- Pro: full history, market comparison, exports, pricing data
- One CTA: **"Start for free"**

### Section 6 — Social Proof / Trust
- If no testimonials yet: use data credibility instead
- "X listings tracked", "12 districts covered", "Updated every 48 hours"
- Clean stat cards in a row

### Section 7 — Final CTA
- Dark section, headline: "Your next investment decision starts here."
- Single button: **"Get started — it's free"**

---

## Design Principles
- **Data-forward:** the product IS the data — show it, don't just describe it
- **Light and clean in content sections**, dark for hero and final CTA
- **No clutter:** wide margins, generous whitespace, max 2 font weights per section
- **Interactive over static:** wherever possible, the visitor should be able to touch/explore something rather than just read about it
- **Trust through precision:** use real-looking numbers (not "10,000+ listings" if you have 8,700 — use 8,700)
